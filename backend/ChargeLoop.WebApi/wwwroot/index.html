❓ DRIVER GUIDE
How ChargeLoop Works
Swap your EV battery in 4 quick steps.
🔄 Battery Swap Steps
1
Find a Nearby Store
Browse live stores on Home and tap 🔋 Batteries to check ready stock.
2
Reserve Your Battery
Tap Reserve to hold a charged battery for 2 hours (cancel anytime).
3
Request Swap & Review Fare
Open Scan & Pay, check the fare breakup, and tap Confirm Swap.
4
Share Your 6-Digit Swap OTP
Show the OTP to the store partner to collect your charged battery.

⚡ Swap Desk Workflow
1
Select or Validate Driver
Tap a request in Requests or search by phone/name.
2
Select Batteries
Confirm the returning battery (blank on first pickup) and the Active battery to issue.
3
Verify Driver's Swap OTP

4
Collect Cash & Complete
Confirm payment to complete the swap, credit earnings, and send the GST bill.
