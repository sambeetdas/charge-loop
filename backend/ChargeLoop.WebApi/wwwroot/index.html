❓ DRIVER GUIDE & WORKFLOW
How ChargeLoop Works for Drivers
Complete walkthrough of every screen, button, and swap step.
🔄 End-to-End Battery Swap Workflow
1. Browse & Preview (Home): View nearby swap stations using your location. Tap 🔋 Batteries on any station card to inspect Active charged batteries before reserving.
2. Reserve (2-Hour Hold): Tap Reserve to hold an Active battery for 2 hours. You can cancel anytime using Reverse Reserve.
3. Scan & Pay (Create Swap Request): Open Scan & Pay (only your Reserved Station is shown).
  • First Battery Pickup: Leave the Depleted Battery field empty.
  • Replacement Swap: Your currently assigned battery serial is prefilled automatically.
  • Review the DB-driven Fare Breakup (Base Fee + GST) and tap Confirm Swap & Record Payment.
4. Share Swap OTP: A 6-digit Swap OTP is generated on screen (tap 🔄 Regenerate OTP if needed). Share it with the Partner at the station. Once the Partner verifies your OTP and confirms, your swap is completed!
💳 Wallet, Payments, Referrals & Bills
• Wallet: Starts at ₹0.00. Record Cash Top-Ups in the Wallet tab.
• Payment Modes: Cash Only is active. Wallet checkout, Card, and UPI are marked WIP (Work in Progress).
• Refer & Earn: Share your CL-... code or claim a friend's code for ₹50 wallet bonus each.
• Bills: View GST tax invoices (INV-CL-...) and tax breakup in the Bills tab.
👤 Profile, PIN & KYC
• Edit your Full Name, Email, and Language in Profile.
• Update your mobile number anytime using your PIN in Change Phone Number.
• Complete KYC (Driving License & Aadhaar) and subscribe to Weekly Plans from Profile.
• Use Forgot / Reset PIN? on the Sign In screen if you ever forget your 4–6 digit PIN.
❓ PARTNER / VENDOR GUIDE
How ChargeLoop Works for Partners
Store setup, 4-status battery control, Swap Desk, and commissions.
🏪 Stores & 4-Status Battery Lifecycle (Stores Tab)
• Add Store: Enter Store Name & Street Address and tap 📍 Find to auto-locate coordinates via Geocoding API.
• Register Battery: Select your Store, enter Battery Serial/QR, pick a DB-configured Battery Type, and enter SOC %.
• 4 Battery Statuses (Toggle Anytime):
  1. Active: Visible to drivers and assignable during swaps.
  2. Inactive: Hidden from drivers and cannot be assigned.
  3. Charging: Hidden from drivers while charging.
  4. Out of Order: Hidden from drivers and blocked from assignment.
⚡ 5-Step Swap Desk Workflow (Swap Desk Tab)
Step 1 — Validate Driver: Search by Driver phone, name, or ID.
Step 2 — Scan Battery QRs: Confirm the returning Depleted Battery QR (empty for first-time pickup) and the Active Charged Battery to issue.
Step 3 — Verify Driver's Swap OTP: Enter the 6-digit Swap OTP shown on the Driver's Scan & Pay screen and tap Verify OTP.
Step 4 — Collect Payment & Execute Swap: On OTP verification, the exact Cash Collected (₹) and full Fare Breakup unlock. Collect Cash and tap Confirm Swap & Collect Payment to complete the Driver's request.
Step 5 — Publish Bill: Send the GST invoice summary across WhatsApp, SMS, or Email.
💰 Earnings & Profile
• Partner Commission Wallet: Starts at ₹0.00. Every completed swap credits your commission instantly to the Earnings ledger.
• Profile: Edit Name, Business Name, Email, UPI ID, and Bank Account, or change your phone number using your PIN.
